FAQ

Common questions.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes! We bring everything we need — pressure washers, cleaning supplies, mowers, trimmers, and all products. The only thing we ask is access to your outdoor water hose for power washing and window cleaning.

How do I schedule a service?

Fill out the form above, give us a call, or text us. We'll get back to you within 24 hours to set up a time that works for you.

Do you offer recurring services?

Absolutely! We offer weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly plans for landscaping and can set up regular power washing and window cleaning schedules too.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Holland, PA and everywhere within a 10-mile radius. Check the map above or contact us to confirm we cover your location.

What's your cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. No fees for cancellations made with proper notice.
